SPROUTS v. 82
Parallel processes for the computer players (Monte Carlo, Parity search). The default is what this machine has.
1 … 9: a new game with that many spots in the chosen layout (one spot in the center). 0: a blank window, spots placed by hand.
Player 1 moves first. Random: any legal move. Monte Carlo: the move after which the set number of random games goes best. Parity search: the move that keeps the number of moves left at the parity it wants (odd; even in misère), looking the set number of moves ahead — or as far as the seconds allow, one move deeper at a time. Filling in one of a pair of fields empties the other. The computer moves as soon as it is its turn; Esc interrupts its thinking, Human in its menu stops it.
Code
Play against someone on another computer. One of you clicks Host; the three-digit code appears in the field, and the other types it in and clicks Join. The host is Player 1 and starts the games; either may undo (U takes back the last move, whoever made it). Only the moves travel: each computer draws its own picture, with its own settings, colors, A and R. Your page is gray while it is not your turn.
Not connected.
When a move does not fit, the status line says why and offers M to make room: by sliding spots along their curves, else by pushing curves apart. Ctrl+click where a spot fits adds one (no curves, 3 lives; not a move). Ctrl+drag moves a spot: along its curve, anywhere in its region (no curves), or back along / onward from its one curve.
Adjusting lays the curves out again with the spots where they are (a spot on its move's curve moves with it); Esc stops it. Each region gets room in proportion to its lives; a region with no move left gets none.
Rearranging moves the spots too. Same position; U undoes it. When a move cannot be made even after making room, R rearranges and tries it again.
Heroic: a curve you draw by hand is kept exactly as drawn, without smoothing; it only may not cross or touch anything. Curves made by clicking are routed and smoothed as before. Curves and spots are not moved locally (no M, no shifting, no Ctrl+drag); A and R still work.
